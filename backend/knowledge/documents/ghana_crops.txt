Ghana Major Crops and Growing Regions

Maize: Grown primarily in the Brong-Ahafo, Ashanti, Eastern, and Volta regions.
Planting season: March to May (major season) and August to October (minor season).
Harvest: 3-4 months after planting.
Key varieties: Obatanpa, Aburohoma, Mamaba, and Golden Jubilee.

Cassava: Major crop in almost all regions. Leading producer: Central Region.
Planting season: Year-round with peak in March-April.
Harvest: 10-18 months after planting.
Key varieties: Afisiafi, Abasa-fitaa, and Gblemoduade.

Cocoa: Main cash crop. Grown in Ashanti, Western, Western North, Central, Eastern, and Volta regions.
Main crop season: October to March. Light crop season: May to August.
Key varieties: Amazonia, Amelonado, and hybrid varieties.

Rice: Grown in Volta, Northern, Upper East, Upper West, and parts of Ashanti.
Major season: April to June. Minor season: September to November.
Key varieties: Jasmine 85, AGRA, Togo Marshall, and Digang.

Yam: Mainly in Northern, Brong-Ahafo, Eastern, and Volta regions.
Planting season: October to February.
Harvest: 6-9 months after planting.
Key varieties: Pona, Dente, Serwah, and Lila.

Groundnuts: Grown in Northern, Upper East, Upper West, and parts of Brong-Ahafo.
Planting season: April to July.
Harvest: 3-4 months after planting.
Key varieties: Chinese, Manipintar, and F-mix.

Tomato: Grown in Upper East, Northern, Brong-Ahafo, Central, and Greater Accra.
Major season: October to February. Minor season: June to August.
Key varieties: Power, Pectomech, and Roma VF.

Palm Oil: Grown in Western, Eastern, Central, and parts of Ashanti.
Palm fruits mature 30-36 months after planting.
Key varieties: Tenera, Dura, and Pisifera.

Ghana Soil Types by Region

Forest zones (Ashanti, Western, Eastern): Ochrosols and Oxysols - well-drained, moderate fertility.
Coastal savanna (Greater Accra, Central, Volta): Sandy loams and clays - low organic matter.
Guinea savanna (Northern, Upper East, Upper West): Loamy sands, prone to erosion.
Transition zone (Brong-Ahafo): Forest-savanna mosaic soils - moderately fertile.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Ghana

Maize: Apply NPK 15-15-15 at 200 kg/ha at planting, top-dress with sulfate of ammonia at 125 kg/ha 4 weeks later.
Cassava: Apply NPK 14-14-14 at 200 kg/ha after planting.
Cocoa: Apply recommended cocoa fertilizer (NPK 0-22-18 + 9CaO + 7MgO) at 300 kg/ha annually.
Rice: Apply NPK 15-15-15 at 150 kg/ha at transplanting, top-dress with urea at 75 kg/ha.
Yam: Apply NPK 15-15-15 at 300 kg/ha, split application at planting and 2 months later.
Vegetables: Apply NPK 15-15-15 at 200 kg/ha plus compost at 5 tons/ha.
