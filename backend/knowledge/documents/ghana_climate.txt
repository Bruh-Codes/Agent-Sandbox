Ghana Climate and Agro-Ecological Zones

== OVERVIEW ==
Ghana lies between latitudes 4°N-12°N and longitudes 1.5°E-3.5°W. Tropical climate modulated by the Intertropical Discontinuity (ITD), which drives distinct wet and dry seasons.

== AGRO-ECOLOGICAL ZONES (Updated 2023 Classification) ==
A 2023 PLOS Climate study by Yamba et al. reclassified Ghana's traditional 4 zones into 5 zones reflecting climate change:

1. SUDAN SAVANNA ZONE (newly delineated)
   - Location: Upper East Region, parts of Upper West
   - Rainfall: Unimodal, 600-900 mm/year
   - Growing season: 90-120 days (May-September)
   - Temperature: 28-34°C
   - Vegetation: Short grasses, drought-resistant shrubs
   - Major crops: Millet, sorghum, groundnut, cowpea

2. GUINEA SAVANNA ZONE (expanded southward)
   - Location: Northern, Savannah, North East, Upper West, parts of Bono
   - Rainfall: Unimodal, 900-1200 mm/year (April-October)
   - Growing season: 120-150 days
   - Temperature: 26-32°C
   - Note: Has expanded southward, engulfing parts of the former transition zone
   - Major crops: Maize, rice, yam, groundnut, cowpea, sorghum, millet

3. TRANSITION ZONE (shrunk in size)
   - Location: Bono, Bono East, Ahafo, parts of Ashanti
   - Rainfall: Bimodal transitioning to unimodal, 1200-1400 mm/year
   - Major rains: April-July, Minor rains: September-October
   - Growing season: 150-200 days
   - Temperature: 25-30°C
   - Note: Zone has shriveled as Guinea Savannah moves south
   - Major crops: Maize, yam, cassava, cashew, tobacco

4. FOREST ZONE (shifted northwest)
   - Location: Western, Western North, Ashanti, Eastern, Central
   - Rainfall: Bimodal, 1400-1750 mm/year
   - Major rains: March-July, Minor rains: September-November
   - Growing season: Year-round
   - Temperature: 24-28°C
   - Humidity: 80-90% mornings, <80% afternoons
   - Vegetation: Semi-deciduous and evergreen forest
   - Note: Zone has shrunk with northwest shift
   - Major crops: Cocoa, oil palm, plantain, cocoyam, coffee, rubber

5. COASTAL ZONE (expanded along entire coast)
   - Location: Greater Accra, Central coast, Volta coast, Western coast
   - Rainfall: Bimodal, 600-900 mm/year (driest zone)
   - Major rains: March-July, Minor rains: August-October
   - Temperature: 25-32°C
   - Vegetation: Coastal savanna with short grasses, scattered palm trees
   - Note: Expanded to encompass all of Ghana's coast
   - Major crops: Vegetables (tomato, pepper, onion), cassava, rice

== RAINFALL PATTERNS ==
Southern Ghana: Bimodal rainfall pattern
- Major rainy season: March-July
- Minor rainy season: September-November
- Dry season: December-February (Harmattan, cool and dusty)

Northern Ghana: Unimodal rainfall pattern
- Rainy season: May-October
- Dry season: November-April (long, hot, dusty)

Onset of rains:
- Southern Ghana: March-April
- Middle Belt: April-May
- Northern Ghana: May-June

== TEMPERATURE ==
- Average annual: 26-29°C
- Peak temperatures: March (hottest month)
- Lowest temperatures: August (coolest month)
- Projected increase: +1.5°C by end of century (RCP 4.5 scenario), reaching ~29.3°C average

== CLIMATE CHANGE TRENDS (GMet Ghana Climate Atlas) ==
1. Rising temperatures year-round, most increase during dry season
2. Total annual rainfall expected to increase ~1.8% but with greater variability
3. Seasonal shifts: AMJ (April-June) expected to decline 1.7%
   JAS (July-September) expected to increase 13.9%
   SON (September-November) expected to increase 3.6%
4. Sea level rise: 0.4-0.7m along Ghana's coastline
5. More extreme events: Floods, droughts, wildfires, coastal erosion

== DROUGHT FREQUENCY ==
- Northern Region: 3-4 events per decade
- Middle Belt: 2-3 events per decade
- Coastal Zone: 1-2 events per decade
- Severity classifications: Mild (15-30% below avg), Moderate (30-50%), Severe (>50%)

== CONSECUTIVE DRY DAYS ==
- Northern Ghana: 50-120 days
- Middle Belt: 30-60 days
- Coastal Areas: 20-45 days
- Peak dry period: December-February

== WET DAYS ==
- Coastal Zone: 80-100 wet days/year
- Forest Zone: 110-150 wet days/year
- Savannah Zone: 70-90 wet days/year
- Peak months: June-July (15-20 wet days per month)

== WIND PATTERNS ==
- Wet season: Moist Southwesterly winds
- Dry season: Dry, dusty Northeasterly winds (Harmattan, December-February)

== 2025 SEASONAL FORECAST (GMet) ==
Major season (Southern Ghana):
- Early onset of rainfall predicted
- High cumulative rainfall amounts
- Early cessation of rains
- Long dry spells between rain events
- Result: Significant shifts from known standard cropping calendars (by weeks in some districts)

2025 actual observations (FAO GIEWS, August 2025):
- Mixed performance across regions
- Brong-Ahafo and Ashanti: Dry spells in July affected crops
- Upper West and Northern: Dry spells affected planting and crop establishment
- Northern areas showed poor vegetation conditions as of end July 2025
- September forecast: Below-average rainfall expected in most regions

== AGRO-METEOROLOGICAL ADVISORIES ==
MoFA and GMet collaboration (under FSRP) produces:
- District-specific cropping calendars
- Real-time weather advisories
- Customized for 2025 major season deviations
- 20 automated weather stations installed for accurate location-specific forecasts
