Ghana Comprehensive Crop Calendar and Planting Guide

== OVERVIEW ==
Ghana has two main rainfall regimes that define the agricultural calendar:
- Forest/Southern zone: Bimodal rainfall (March-July and September-November)
- Northern/Savanna zone: Unimodal rainfall (May-October)
The transition zone (Brong-Ahafo) has a mix of both patterns.

== MAJOR CEREALS ==

=== Maize (Zea mays) ===
- Growing regions: Brong-Ahafo (leading), Ashanti, Eastern, Volta, Northern
- Major season planting: March-May (rains begin)
- Major season harvest: July-August (3-4 months)
- Minor season planting: August-October (September rains)
- Minor season harvest: November-January
- Improved varieties: Obatanpa (GH110-5), Aburohoma, Mamaba, Golden Jubilee, Omankwa, Dorke SR
- Maturity: 90-120 days depending on variety
- Spacing: 80cm x 40cm (2 seeds/hole)
- Seed rate: 20-25 kg/ha for improved varieties
- Expected yield (improved): 3.5-5.5 t/ha under good management
- Expected yield (local): 1.0-1.5 t/ha
- Note: Obatanpa is the most popular improved variety, known for high yield and tolerance to low soil fertility

=== Rice (Oryza sativa) ===
- Growing regions: Volta (largest), Northern, Upper East, Upper West, Ashanti
- Major season planting: April-June
- Major season harvest: August-October
- Minor season planting: September-November (irrigated areas)
- Minor season harvest: January-March
- Improved varieties: Jasmine 85, AGRA rice, Togo Marshall, Digang, Sikamo, Ex-Baika
- Maturity: 110-135 days
- Planting method: Transplanting (21-28 day seedlings) or direct seeding
- Seed rate (transplanting): 40-50 kg/ha
- Seed rate (direct seeding): 80-120 kg/ha
- Expected yield (irrigated): 4.0-6.0 t/ha
- Expected yield (rainfed): 1.5-3.0 t/ha
- Main production areas: Ada East (Volta), Nasia Valley (Northern region)
- Government priority crop under Planting for Food and Jobs (PFJ)

=== Millet (Pennisetum glaucum) ===
- Growing regions: Upper East, Upper West, Northern
- Planting season: May-July (with early rains)
- Harvest season: September-October
- Maturity: 90-120 days
- Key varieties: Nara, Kpari, Zaa
- Drought-tolerant, ideal for northern savanna zones

=== Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) ===
- Growing regions: Upper East, Upper West, Northern
- Planting season: May-July
- Harvest season: September-November
- Maturity: 100-150 days
- Key varieties: Kapaala, Naga White, Dorado
- Uses: Food (TZ, porridge), brewing (pito), livestock feed

== ROOT AND TUBER CROPS ==

=== Cassava (Manihot esculenta) ===
- Growing regions: Central (leading), Eastern, Volta, Brong-Ahafo, Northern
- Planting season: Year-round, peak March-May
- Harvest: 10-18 months after planting (can be left in ground)
- Improved varieties: Afisiafi, Abasa-fitaa (Bankyehemaa), Gblemoduade, Essam Bankye, Nkabom
- Maturity: 12-18 months for improved varieties
- Spacing: 100cm x 100cm
- Planting material: Stem cuttings 25-30cm long
- Expected yield: 15-30 t/ha (improved), 8-12 t/ha (local)
- Note: Ghana is among the top 5 global cassava producers
- Major processing: Gari, fufu flour, starch, ethanol

=== Yam (Dioscorea spp.) ===
- Growing regions: Northern (leading), Brong-Ahafo, Eastern, Volta
- Planting season: October-February (requires dry season for maturation)
- Major harvest: July-November (6-9 months)
- Improved varieties: Pona (D. alata), Dente, Serwah, Lila, Kukrupa, Matches
- Maturity: 6-10 months depending on variety
- Seed yam rate: 1.5-2.5 t/ha (yam setts)
- Expected yield: 12-20 t/ha (improved), 8-12 t/ha (local)
- Spacing: 100cm x 100cm for ware yams
- Ghana is the 2nd largest yam producer in the world after Nigeria
- Main species: D. alata (white yam, water yam), D. rotundata (yellow yam)
- Storage: Can be stored 3-6 months in well-ventilated barns

=== Cocoyam (Colocasia esculenta & Xanthosoma sagittifolium) ===
- Growing regions: Ashanti, Eastern, Volta, Central
- Planting season: March-June
- Harvest: 8-12 months after planting
- Expected yield: 6-10 t/ha
- Often intercropped in cocoa and palm plantations

== CASH CROPS ==

=== Cocoa (Theobroma cacao) ===
- Growing regions: Western North (leading), Western, Ashanti, Central, Eastern, Volta, Brong-Ahafo
- Main crop season: October-March (peak harvest)
- Light crop (mid-crop) season: May-August
- Key varieties: Amazonia (hybrid), Amelonado, Upper Amazon, Inter-Upper Amazon
- Maturity: First pods at 18-24 months, full production at 4-5 years
- Expected yield: 400-600 kg/ha (traditional), 800-1200 kg/ha (with good management)
- Spacing: 3m x 3m (1111 trees/ha)
- Shade trees required: Gliricidia, Terminalia, Plantain
- Ghana is the 2nd largest cocoa producer in the world (after Cote d'Ivoire)
- Major diseases: Black pod disease, Swollen shoot virus, Mistletoe
- Major pests: Mirids (capsids), Cocoa pod borer
- CODAPEC program provides free spraying of fungicides/insecticides
- Cocoa price is regulated by COCOBOD annually

=== Oil Palm (Elaeis guineensis) ===
- Growing regions: Western, Eastern, Central, Ashanti
- Planting season: March-September
- First harvest: 30-36 months after planting
- Peak production: 7-12 years
- Economic life: 20-30 years
- Varieties: Dura (thick shell), Pisifera (thin shell), Tenera (Dura x Pisifera hybrid, best for oil)
- Tenera yields 4-7 t/ha of crude palm oil vs 1.5-2 t/ha for Dura
- Expected yield: 15-25 t/ha fresh fruit bunches (improved tenera)

=== Cashew (Anacardium occidentale) ===
- Growing regions: Brong-Ahafo, Northern, Upper East, Central
- Planting season: April-June
- First harvest: 3 years after planting
- Full production: 7-10 years
- Harvest season: February-May
- Expected yield: 500-1500 kg/ha raw nuts
- Ghana exported ~150,000+ tonnes of raw cashew nuts annually

=== Cotton (Gossypium spp.) ===
- Growing regions: Northern, Upper East, Upper West
- Planting season: May-July
- Harvest: November-December
- Maturity: 120-150 days
- Key varieties: Bt cotton approved in 2020

=== Coffee (Coffea canephora - Robusta) ===
- Growing regions: Western, Ashanti, Brong-Ahafo, Volta
- Planting season: March-May
- First harvest: 3 years after planting
- Harvest season: October-December
- Mostly Robusta variety

== VEGETABLES ==

=== Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) ===
- Growing regions: Upper East (leading), Brong-Ahafo, Central, Greater Accra
- Major season planting: September-November (for December-February harvest)
- Major season harvest: December-March
- Minor season planting: May-July
- Minor season harvest: July-September
- Key varieties: Power, Pectomech, Roma VF, Tropimech
- Maturity: 75-90 days from transplanting
- Expected yield: 10-20 t/ha

=== Onion (Allium cepa) ===
- Growing regions: Upper East (most important), Upper West
- Planting season: September-November (dry season cultivation with irrigation)
- Harvest: December-March
- Key varieties: Bawku Red, Texas Early Grano, Prema, Red Creole
- Maturity: 3-5 months
- Expected yield: 10-20 t/ha

=== Pepper (Capsicum spp.) ===
- Growing regions: Central, Greater Accra, Volta, Ashanti
- Planting season: Year-round with irrigation
- Maturity: 60-90 days after transplanting
- Varieties: Scotch Bonnet, Kpakpo Shito, Bird Pepper, Cayenne, Bell pepper
- Expected yield: 8-12 t/ha (chili), 15-25 t/ha (bell pepper)

=== Plantain (Musa paradisiaca) ===
- Growing regions: Ashanti, Central, Eastern, Western, Volta
- Planting season: March-June
- Harvest: 8-12 months after planting
- Key varieties: Apenkwa (Apantu), Oniaba, Corne 1
- Spacing: 3m x 2m
- Expected yield: 8-15 t/ha

=== Okro (Abelmoschus esculentus) ===
- Growing regions: All regions, especially Northern, Upper East, Greater Accra
- Planting season: March-August
- Maturity: 50-70 days
- Expected yield: 5-8 t/ha

=== Pepper, Eggplant, Cabbage, Lettuce ===
- Grown in all regions, especially peri-urban areas near Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi
- Dry season production uses irrigation from dams and shallow wells

== LEGUMES ==

=== Groundnut (Arachis hypogaea) ===
- Growing regions: Northern, Upper East, Upper West, Brong-Ahafo
- Planting season: April-July (early-mid rains)
- Harvest: 3-4 months after planting (August-October)
- Varieties: Chinese, Manipintar, F-mix, Obolo, Adepa
- Expected yield: 1.0-2.5 t/ha (unshelled)

=== Cowpea (Vigna unguiculata) ===
- Growing regions: Northern, Upper East, Upper West, Brong-Ahafo
- Planting season: June-August (relay cropping after cereals)
- Harvest: September-October
- Varieties: Asontem, Soronko, Hewale, Tona
- Maturity: 70-90 days
- Expected yield: 0.8-1.5 t/ha

=== Soybean (Glycine max) ===
- Growing regions: Northern, Brong-Ahafo, Ashanti
- Planting season: June-August
- Harvest: October-November
- Varieties: Jenguma, Salintuy, Anidaso
- Maturity: 95-115 days

=== Bambara Groundnut (Vigna subterranea) ===
- Grown in Northern, Upper East, Upper West regions
- Very drought-tolerant
- Planted May-July, harvested October-December

== INTERCROPPING SYSTEMS ==
Common traditional intercropping patterns:
- Maize + Cassava + Plantain (forest zone)
- Yam + Maize + Vegetables (transition zone)
- Millet/Sorghum + Groundnut/Cowpea (savanna zone)
- Cocoa + Plantain/Cocoyam (young cocoa farms)

== LIVESTOCK INTEGRATION ==
- Small ruminants (sheep, goats) are common in northern regions
- Poultry production is widespread (mostly local breeds)
- Cattle are mainly in the Northern, Upper East, Upper West regions
- Pig production is minimal due to religious/cultural factors
